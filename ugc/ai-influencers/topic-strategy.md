## Tier C (Under-performers, <10K views)
- Generic positive affirmations
- "Buenos días" style content

## Key Insight
**Specific pain > generic positive.** Always pick a concrete emotional situation, not a vague feel-good message. The audience comes for validation of their suffering, not cheerfulness.
