## Core Thesis
AI made the 7/10 accessible to everyone. The average IS the new mediocre. Taste lives in the gap between what AI produces and what you actually want.

## Key Frameworks

### 1. Generate, Then Destroy
- Produce 10-20 versions of anything
- Not to pick the best — to develop a REJECTION vocabulary
- "This fails because X" (X must be structural, not cosmetic)
- Do it enough → you internalize a framework you couldn't have articulated before

### 2. The Specificity Test
- "Could this have been written about anything else?"
- If yes → it has no taste
- Taste is irreducibly contextual (Rick Rubin's Californication example)

### 3. Resistance as Signal
- When AI output bothers you and you can't say why → DON'T move on
- Sit with the friction — that's the raw material of taste
- Over time: unexplained discomfort → aesthetic immune system

### 4. Taste Triad
- **Curiosity:** Not liking inspiration — interrogating it
- **Judgment:** Selection AND rejection simultaneously
- **Standards:** Lines you won't cross. If you don't have them, AI supplies "culturally averaged aesthetics"

### 5. Find the Canon, Then Ignore the Canon
- Use AI to map best work in any field across 100-300 years
- Read/watch/absorb it
- Understand WHY it worked there, not to copy it

## The 75-25 Split
- 75% and below → AI slop
- Above 75% → masterpieces
- AI raised the floor but the ceiling is higher than ever
- Everyone can ship a 7/10 — stand out requires 9/10 in judgment, selection, specificity

## Daily 10-Min Practice
1. Pick one output → generate 10 versions with AI
2. Write "fails because..." for each
3. Rewrite one version with ONE constraint (no adjectives, no gradients, one idea)
4. Publish
